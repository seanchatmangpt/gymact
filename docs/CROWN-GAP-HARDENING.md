# Crown Gap Hardening

GymAct owns bounded worlds, lawful consequences, independent observation, verification, and replayable evidence. It does not own planning or semantic manufacture.

Hard laws:

- world affordances are derived mechanically from executable objects;
- ReAct/DSPy may select among lawful moves but cannot grant DO authority;
- every consequential effect crosses an injected receipt-bearing port;
- generated world/task projections must be accepted by the exact production toolchain before acquiring standing;
- missing or stale generated projections fail closed rather than being covered by a raised baseline.
